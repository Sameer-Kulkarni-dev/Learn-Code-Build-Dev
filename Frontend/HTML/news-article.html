Bengaluru, October 11, 2026

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is helping students learn in new ways. Many schools use AI tools to give personalized lessons and help students understand difficult topics. Teachers also use AI to check assignments and track student progress. This saves time and improves learning. Experts believe AI will play an important role in education, but they also stress the need for safe and responsible use of technology.
